# Magic Springs Theme and Water Park (Hot Springs, Arkansas)

Magic Springs opened July 22, 1978 as "Magic Springs Family Fun Park," struggled with debt, closed in 1995, and reopened May 27, 2000 with the Crystal Falls water park after Hot Springs voters approved a bond issue. Owners and operators churned (Themeparks LLC, CNL, PARC, Amusement Management Partners) until EPR Properties bought it (2016–17) with Premier Parks, LLC as operator, which is still the arrangement in 2026. As of 2026 it has about 38 attractions, the Timberwood Amphitheater (5,000 seats, 2003), and a retired-but-standing wooden coaster (Arkansas Twister). This file feeds Spa City Thrills (original theme-park sim inspired by Magic Springs) and trivia; no logos or brand assets were collected and game rules must be original.

## Incidents

## Game hooks

For **Spa City Thrills** (original theme-park sim *inspired by* Magic Springs — invent park, ride and company names; use real history only as tuning data):
